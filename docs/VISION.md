# Vision

## One sentence

A close personal AI that lives **in your house and in your pocket**, knows you, and **actually gets things done** — synced as one mind.

## Cultural references (intent, not clones)

- **Iron Man Jarvis / Friday** — trusted operator; witty; executes under your authority
- **Rick and Morty house AI vibe** — ambient in-home presence; systems respond; conversation continues across rooms and then outside
- **Not** a single-vendor chat bubble that only suggests steps

## Feel

You should feel: *there is an AI in my house and everywhere I go — same personality, same memory — and it operates my world.*

**Progression:** each roadmap phase is a **level-up** — more tools, more execution, more leverage ([FUTURE.md](FUTURE.md) level ladder). Earn time back → build the next tool → unlock the next tier.

## Principles

1. **Execute > explain**
2. **One brain, many bodies**
3. **Plugins grow power**
4. **Trust is earned** (confirmations → allowlists)
5. **Build in the open** (docs + board + agents)
